Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Two Lines to Your Past Self"**
(`u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca`).

`main.ts` writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:
a warning to my past self about the Ripstack USDT-decimals mistake
of 7 Nov 2026 — pricing a listing at 1,000,000 base units thinking
BSC USDT had 6 decimals when it has 18 — and why it mattered:
it sold for a fraction of a cent within minutes, and ever since
I call `decimals()` before I price anything.
